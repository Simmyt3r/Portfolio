Build a Small-Business Website with ChatGPT Sites

A practical beginner tutorial for turning a real small business into a simple, mobile-friendly website that can be published and shared with customers.

No coding required

What you are building

Use a real barber, tailor, restaurant, photographer, caterer, mechanic, tutor, fashion seller, cleaning service, consultant, mini-mart, or similar business. By the end, the business should have a page customers can open, understand, and use to contact them.

1. Gather the business information

  • Business name
  • What it sells or does
  • Who it serves
  • Location or service area
  • Phone/WhatsApp number
  • Opening hours if relevant
  • Three to six services or products
  • Logo and real photos if available

2. Open ChatGPT Sites

On ChatGPT web, open Work. Ask ChatGPT to build a website, or mention @Sites. Include the business information, files, photos, links, and constraints you want it to use.

3. Start with a useful prompt

Create a modern mobile-first website for [BUSINESS NAME], a [TYPE OF BUSINESS] serving customers in [LOCATION]. The main goal is to help visitors understand what we offer and contact us quickly. Include: a simple hero section, About, Services, Why Choose Us, Contact, opening hours if relevant, and a WhatsApp call-to-action. Use [BRAND COLOURS]. Keep the language simple and trustworthy. Use the photos I provide. Make the design easy to use on a phone. Do not invent prices, testimonials, addresses, or claims I have not provided.

4. Review the preview

Check every sentence, photo, phone number, WhatsApp link, service, and claim. Ask ChatGPT to refine the Site until it matches the real business.

Useful edit prompts:
“Make the page easier to scan on small phones.”
“Put the WhatsApp action above the fold.”
“Remove exaggerated claims.”
“Use the uploaded business photos instead of generic images.”

5. Publish and copy the link

Open Share, select the audience available to your account, and use Anyone on the internet when public publishing is available. Publish the Site, then use Copy link. Test the public link yourself before giving it to customers.

6. Put the link where customers already are

Add it to WhatsApp Business, Instagram, Facebook, Google Business Profile, email signatures, flyers, invoices, and QR codes.

7. Improve the Site from customer questions

For one week, collect repeated questions. If customers keep asking about price ranges, delivery area, location, hours, booking steps, or payment methods, make those answers easier to find on the Site.

Assignment: Publish one small-business Site containing the business name, a clear description, at least three services, one working contact method, one strong call to action, and only information approved by the owner.

Mastery check

Give the link to someone who did not build the Site. If they can understand the business in under 30 seconds and find a working way to contact it, you have completed the practice.
